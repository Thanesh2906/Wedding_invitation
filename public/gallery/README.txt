Add your wedding photos here to fill the "Our Moments" gallery.

Name them 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg, 04.jpg, 05.jpg, 06.jpg (matching
data/wedding.json > gallery.photos), or edit that file to point at
whatever filenames/paths you use instead.

Until a photo exists at a given path, that gallery frame automatically
shows an ornamental monogram placeholder instead of a broken image, so
it's safe to deploy before all six photos are ready.

Recommended: square or portrait JPGs, ~1500px on the long edge, under
~500KB each, so the gallery stays fast to load.
